A lot of teams we talk to have already taken their first steps with AI: they've signed up for Claude, rolled it out to the whole team, and encourage them to use it "as much as possible". However, there's often gap between having access to a set of powerful AI tools and being able to get great results from them.

That was exactly where this small, 8-person team found themselves. This architectural company* had a paid Claude subscription in hand, but felt they were only scratching the surface of what it had to offer, particularly with Claude Cowork. The enthusiasm was there, they just needed a bridge to fill the skills gap. 

That's where AI With Ease came in.

## What we did

After speaking with the team's manager, we determined that Claude Skills would be a good starting point. What followed was hands-on, 60 minute workshop delivered online for the team to attend. Rather than a generic "here's what AI can do" session, the workshop was built around the team's day-to-day work and what they were likely to face in their own week. We walked through:

* What Claude skills are and how they work
* How to set up and apply skills for more consistent and higher-quality outputs in Claude Cowork specifically
* A practical exercise for the team to create their very own first skill and put it to work immediately

Because the group ranged from AI beginners to some who had already been experimenting on their own, the session was paced to bring everyone to the same page. Some brief fundamentals flowed into more advanced techniques so that nobody felt left behind or held back.

## What the team took away

The feedback afterwards was some of the most rewarding we've received. The team described the workshop as genuinely practical, mixing theory with actionable content. Most importantly, they didn't feel like AI was about to replace them; rather, they felt "empowered to make even more of an impact in their work hours". They could see how they would have more time for the things that mattered, and less on what was draining. Several people commented on the clarity of the contents, and how professionally the session was run.

## Why this matters

This is the kind of outcome we love to see. AI shouldn't feel overwhelming, and it shouldn't be left unused simply because noone has had the chance to learn how to use it fully. With the right guidance, having AI goes from checking a box to being a key asset for a team of professionals. 

That's the whole ethos behind AI With Ease: AI lends a hand, people lead the way.
